This draft describes the inspected Android game with Google AdMob rewarded advertisements. Desktop versions do not initialize this Android ad integration.

Game progress and accounts

The game stores progress and settings on your device. It does not provide game-account creation or account login. Local progress is separate from the information processed by advertising services. Clearing the app's storage in Android removes its local saves and settings, but does not delete information already processed by an advertising service. Device backup and restore settings may also affect local data.

Advertising data

The Android version integrates Google's Mobile Ads SDK to provide rewarded advertisements. Google's SDK disclosure identifies these types of data:

  • IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Interaction information, such as launches, taps, and ad-video views.
  • Diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.
  • Identifiers, including advertising ID and app set ID, and where applicable identifiers associated with signed-in device accounts.

Google describes collection and sharing for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The purposes applicable to each identifier differ; app set ID must not be used for advertising personalization or measurement.

The inspected game code does not submit a custom player-account ID or request access to personal documents, contacts, calendar entries, photos, microphone recordings, or precise GPS location. This does not mean the ad SDK collects no personal information.

See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, Privacy Policy, and information about partner apps for its data practices and recipients.

Ad choices and consent

Choosing whether to watch a rewarded ad is not the same as choosing whether advertising data is collected. The currently inspected implementation initializes and preloads ads when the Android game starts. It does not yet implement an in-game consent or privacy-options flow. The release must address this before publication; this draft does not promise a working opt-out.

Android offers advertising-ID controls, depending on the device and OS version. Changing those controls does not necessarily stop all SDK collection or erase information previously collected.

Security, retention, and deletion

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. The final release's other dependencies and network paths still need verification; no method of transmission or storage provides absolute security.

Local saves remain until removed or replaced. This draft makes no promise that advertising information is deleted when you close or uninstall the game, or within 90 days. Google's retention practices are described in its retention information. The publisher must confirm the retention and deletion practices applicable to the final integration before this policy is finalized.

The current game has no account-deletion feature or established developer process for deleting SDK-held information. You can contact the privacy email with questions; an email address alone is not a verified SDK-data deletion mechanism. This statement does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law. Applicable rights and the process for exercising them must be finalized for the territories where the game will be offered.
